# Screenshots

Taken on an Android 16 emulator (Pixel 3 profile, 1080 x 2160), 4.0.0.

| Modes menu | Cable release |
|---|---|
| ![modes menu](screenshots/drawer.png) | ![cable release](screenshots/cable-release.png) |

The drawer groups the modes; the red bar carries the status-bar inset on Android 15. Simple
cable release: one tap, one shot.

| Timelapse running | Sound sensor armed |
|---|---|
| ![timelapse](screenshots/timelapse.png) | ![sound sensor](screenshots/sound-sensor.png) |

Timelapse at a 1 s interval with the countdown ring and the shot counter. The sound sensor
shows the microphone level on the dial and the volume warning strip at the bottom.

| Solar calculator | ND calculator |
|---|---|
| ![solar](screenshots/solar.png) | ![nd](screenshots/nd.png) |

The solar calculator for the emulator's location (times in UTC on the emulator). The ND
calculator: 1/8000 s behind a one-stop filter is 1/4000 s. The bottom bar shows the sound
sensor still running from another screen.
